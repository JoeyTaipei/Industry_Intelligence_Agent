# Industry Intelligence Brief

**Demo scenario:** Taiwan semiconductor / AI server industry  
**Audience:** Deloitte Digital Technology intern interview  
**Evidence type:** Sample interview demo data, not final client advice  
**Companies covered:** TSMC, MediaTek, Quanta, Wistron, Foxconn, Cathay Financial, Fubon Financial

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Fact from sources:** The demo evidence covers Taiwan companies across semiconductors, AI server assembly, electronics manufacturing, and financial services.
- **Fact from sources:** Sample news and annual report snippets highlight AI server demand, advanced semiconductor demand, working capital pressure, supply chain risk, export controls, cybersecurity, and ESG/energy topics.
- **Analytical interpretation:** The Taiwan semiconductor / AI server ecosystem appears to have strong growth momentum, but the operating model is exposed to supply chain reliability, customer concentration, inventory planning, geopolitical uncertainty, and cyber/digital risk.
- **Analytical interpretation:** These risks create clear digital transformation opportunities: predictive demand planning, supplier risk monitoring, working capital dashboards, cybersecurity monitoring, and ESG data reporting.
- **Recommended follow-up:** The consulting team should validate which risks are already tracked by management, where data exists, and which digital opportunities can be tied to measurable KPI impact.

### 中文執行摘要

- **來源事實：** 本示範情境涵蓋台灣半導體、AI server 組裝、電子製造與金融服務公司。
- **來源事實：** 樣本新聞與年報片段顯示 AI server 需求、先進半導體需求、營運資金壓力、供應鏈風險、出口管制、資安與 ESG / 能源議題。
- **分析解讀：** 台灣半導體 / AI server 生態系具備成長動能，但也面臨供應鏈穩定性、客戶集中度、庫存規劃、地緣政治與資安風險。
- **分析解讀：** 這些風險可轉化為數位轉型機會，例如需求預測、供應商風險監控、營運資金儀表板、資安監控與 ESG 資料治理。
- **建議追問：** 顧問團隊應驗證管理層目前追蹤哪些風險、資料在哪裡、以及哪些數位機會能連結可衡量 KPI 成效。

## 2. Business Signal Extraction

### Facts from Sources

- AI server production capacity is a recurring theme across Quanta, Wistron, and Foxconn sample news records.
- Advanced semiconductor demand is linked to AI accelerator growth and advanced manufacturing capacity.
- Working capital pressure is connected to inventory planning and shifting enterprise customer order timing.
- Export controls and geopolitical uncertainty appear as important external risk signals.
- Manufacturing digitization increases cybersecurity and production continuity needs.

### Business Implications

- Growth is not only a sales opportunity; it also creates operational complexity.
- AI server demand may require better demand sensing, capacity planning, supplier coordination, and customer order visibility.
- External market news can be used as an early-warning layer, but client interviews and internal data are needed before making recommendations.

### 商業意涵

- 成長不只是營收機會，也會提高營運複雜度。
- AI server 需求需要更好的需求感知、產能規劃、供應商協調與客戶訂單可視化。
- 外部新聞可作為早期風險訊號，但仍需要客戶訪談與內部資料驗證。

### 商業意涵

- KRI Evidence Table 是分析師審閱清單，不是最終風險評分。
- 重複出現的 medium/high 訊號應透過財務資料、營運 KPI 與管理層訪談驗證。
- 供應鏈、庫存與客戶集中度風險，特別適合用來討論 AI server 成長下的營運挑戰。

## 4. Digital Transformation Opportunity Map

| Opportunity Theme | Possible Solution | KPI / KRI Link | Client Interview Question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Predictive demand and capacity planning | AI-assisted demand forecast and capacity planning dashboard | Forecast accuracy, capacity utilization, order volatility | How accurate are current customer forecasts and how often do plans change? |
| Supplier risk monitoring | Supplier delivery delay and shortage early-warning dashboard | Delay rate, shortage count, alternative supplier coverage | Which components create the highest delivery risk? |
| Working capital early warning | Inventory and receivables dashboard | Inventory days, AR days, cash conversion cycle | Which inventory categories create the highest working capital pressure? |
| Cyber and production continuity monitoring | OT/IT cybersecurity and incident dashboard | Security incidents, downtime, recovery time | How are connected factory risks monitored today? |
| ESG and energy reporting | Energy and emissions data platform | Energy intensity, renewable energy ratio, carbon emissions | What ESG metrics are already reliable enough for management reporting? |

## 5. Recommended Client Interview Questions

### Recommended Follow-up

- Which risk themes are already tracked by management, and which are only external market signals?
- Which AI server customers or product lines create the most demand volatility?
- Which components or suppliers create the highest production delay risk?
- What KPIs are reviewed monthly for inventory, receivables, capacity, and delivery performance?
- Which data sources are available today, and which are manual or fragmented?
- What would make a digital solution valuable: lower cost, faster cycle time, better risk visibility, or improved forecasting?

### 建議下一步與客戶訪談問題

- 哪些風險主題已由管理層定期追蹤？哪些只是外部市場訊號？
- 哪些 AI server 客戶或產品線造成最大的需求波動？
- 哪些零組件或供應商造成最高的生產延遲風險？
- 客戶每月檢視哪些庫存、應收帳款、產能與交付 KPI？
- 目前哪些資料可取得？哪些仍是人工整理或分散在不同系統？
- 數位解決方案的價值應如何衡量：降低成本、縮短流程時間、提升風險可視化，或改善預測準確率？
