# Deloitte Interview Demo Scenario

This folder contains a self-contained demo scenario for the Industry Intelligence Agent.

## Scenario

A consulting team wants to quickly understand the Taiwan semiconductor / AI server industry and identify business risks and digital transformation opportunities.

## Files

- `sample_company_registry.csv`: sample company basic information
- `sample_news_articles.csv`: sample business news signals
- `sample_annual_report_snippets.md`: synthetic annual report-style text snippets
- `sample_industry_trend_notes.json`: industry trend notes
- `sample_kri_extraction_results.csv`: KRI Evidence Table
- `dashboard_ready_kpi_kri_table.csv`: dashboard-ready KPI/KRI table
- `final_industry_intelligence_brief.md`: consulting-style final report
- `final_demo_summary_zh_one_page.md`: one-page Traditional Chinese interview summary

## Guardrail

The data is created for interview demonstration. It should be presented as sample evidence, not verified client research.
